Privacy Policy - Estudio YT

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Estudio YT is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is used by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects one of his YouTube channels, Google grants the app access through the YouTube Data API (OAuth). The app uses this access only to:

  • read the connected channel's name, ID and public statistics;
  • upload the owner's own videos as private, with title, description, tags, thumbnail and playlist;
  • update the connected channel's description, keywords and banner when the owner asks for it.

Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's, is sold, or is shared with third parties. The app does not access any other user's data.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Estudio YT's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
